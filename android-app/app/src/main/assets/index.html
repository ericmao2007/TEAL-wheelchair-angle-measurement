Calibration

180° reversal zero · Pitch

Measure once facing uphill, then once facing downhill on the same slope. Keep the wheelchair upright and the sensor mounting unchanged. The two side views show the measurement positions; turn around on the ground between them. Capture only when still.

Each click records the latest Pitch immediately. Zero offset = (first angle + second angle) / 2; readings subtract this offset. No stability, motion, turn, accuracy or calibration checks. The offset stays until you clear it, replace it. It is saved automatically for future launches. IMU calibration is retained.

Wheelchair mounting · level + forward

Keep the sensor fixed in its final mounting. Use an empty wheelchair, secured against rolling. Do not balance or lift an occupied chair.

1. Place all wheels on a known level surface. Hold still for 3 seconds, then record level.

2. Place the chair straight uphill on a secure 5–30° ramp, with no cross slope. Keep every wheel supported and hold still for 3 seconds. Record forward to save. The incline angle need not be known.

This corrects all three mounting rotations: X = forward, Y = left, Z = up. Uphill Pitch is positive. This does not measure compass heading. A side tilt during step 2 causes an incorrect forward direction; a single static pose cannot detect this.

Saved on this phone. Repeat after moving the sensor or changing its sensor calibration. Mounting replaces previous zero corrections; Set zero afterward is an optional relative reference.
